# Category photos

Photos for the 12 listing categories, 4 per category, stored as `<category>/01.png` to `04.png`.
They are **illustrative only** and are not photos of any real rental. Each one is the photo of a
demo listing (`lib/demoListings.json`, added with `npm run seed-demo`).

All 48 were downloaded on 2026-10-24 from Wikimedia Commons. The license of each file was read
from its Commons metadata (`extmetadata`: `License`, `LicenseShortName`, `LicenseUrl`,
`AttributionRequired`, `Restrictions`) at download time. Only files with no listed
restrictions (such as trademark or personality rights) under CC0, public domain, CC BY
or CC BY-SA were accepted. Each image was also checked visually for text, signs,
logos and watermarks.

## Changes made

Every file was downloaded as a Commons rendition, resized to 1280px wide (aspect ratio
kept, no cropping) and re-encoded as PNG. The conversion decodes the image and writes
new PNG data; extensions were not renamed. No other edits were made.

## Attribution requirements

- **CC BY** and **CC BY-SA**: wherever an image is shown, credit the creator, link the
  source page and the license, and say it was changed (resized and converted to PNG).
  Do not imply the creator endorses this project. The "Credit line" column is ready to use.
- **CC BY-SA** additionally: these PNGs are adaptations, so they stay under the same
  CC BY-SA license and may not be given more restrictive terms.
- **Public domain**: no attribution is legally required; credit is kept as a courtesy.
- The app shows each photo's credit on the listing card, the listing page and the
  reservations page, and lists all of them on `/credits` (data in `lib/demoListings.json`).
