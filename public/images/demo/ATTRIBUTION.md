# Site photos

Decorative photos used on the home page banner, empty states, and the add-listing page.
They are **illustrative only** and are not photos of any listed property.

All three were downloaded on 2026-10-24 from Wikimedia Commons, resized to 1920px wide and
saved as JPEG. Their license was read from each file's Commons metadata (`extmetadata`) at
download time, and each was checked visually for text, signs, logos and watermarks.

## Attribution requirements

None: CC0 waives all rights, so no credit is required. The photos are still listed on the
Image credits page (`/credits`, data in `lib/imageCredits.ts`) as a courtesy. No credit
caption is shown next to them.

These replace three CC BY 2.0 photos used earlier. CC BY 2.0 has no clause allowing credit
only on a separate linked page, so they were replaced rather than shown without a caption.
